0. Create development accounts

Optional first-time setup. Upload or paste setup-public.json generated on your computer. This creates six new child accounts: treasury (2.5 testnet NEAR), three reviewers, arbiter and worker (0.1 each). Total: 3 testnet NEAR plus gas. Full-access keys for these children stay on your computer; no key is added to your parent account. All reviewers are controlled by the same tester, not independent operators.



1. Verify your treasury
Upload the public testnet configuration generated for a deployed v0.2 treasury. It must contain account IDs and commitments only, never keys. Bytecode, participants and timing are checked against one final RPC block.
Verification boundary
This trusts the testnet RPC. It is not a light client or a proof of reviewer independence. A connected wallet is not authentication to the private workspace.


2. Prepare an action
Each operation is checked against your wallet role and current contract state. Browser funding is capped at 0.01 testnet NEAR per treasury. Wallet gas is separate.

3. Verify the outcome
